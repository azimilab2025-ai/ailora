## Overview

AILORA is a space decision-support, analysis, and simulation platform designed to provide integrated situational awareness of Earth-orbit objects and activities. It supports human decision-making through governed evidence, explainable analysis, and bounded risk assessment — operating on Physics-First and AI-Advisory-Only principles.

The platform targets LEO, MEO, GEO, and HEO orbital regimes. All AI outputs are strictly advisory; the platform makes no autonomous operational decisions and maintains a permanent prohibition on any spacecraft command, telecommand, uplink, or autonomous maneuver execution path.

The immediate delivery goal is a **Challenge-ready Enterprise SaaS Foundation** paired with a **Verified Space Vertical Slice** demonstrating end-to-end conjunction risk assessment and advisory recommendation.

## Scope

AILORA is scoped exclusively to Earth orbit (`EARTH_ORBIT_ONLY`). The active baseline is terrestrial; on-orbit runtime is deferred.

**Active orbital regimes:** LEO · MEO · GEO · HEO

**Primary capabilities (planned):**
- Space Situational Awareness
- Orbital Object and Debris Tracking
- Conjunction Risk Assessment (Advisory)
- Traffic Coordination Advisory
- Mission Planning Simulation
- AI Advisory with Provenance

**Permanently out of scope:**
- Spacecraft Command
- Telecommand
- Uplink
- Flight Control Execution
- Autonomous Maneuver Execution

These are not "not yet implemented" features — they are **permanently prohibited** under all conditions, modes, and extensions (`E9 / APR-X / INC-0 / HARD_DENY`).

## Domain Review Status

```
PROMPT_06_DOMAIN_REVIEW = OPEN — REQUIRED — NOT_NORMATIVELY_ACTIVATED
```

Prompt 06 (`CSIP-EO-RS-STAGE-20`) carries a standing `DOMAIN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` sentinel. This means that the Astrodynamics and scientific-algorithm contracts defined in Prompt 06 have **not** received the independent qualified scientific review required to achieve Normative or Operational status.

**Effect on the current project:**
- All demonstration algorithms for conjunction risk and collision probability must be presented as **Advisory, Bounded, and non-Normative**.
- No scientific output may be labelled as Normative, Qualified, or Operationally Authoritative until this review is completed through the process defined in Prompt 06.
- This gate is **non-blocking** for project identity and foundation development, but is **blocking** for any Normative scientific claim.
