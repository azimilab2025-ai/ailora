# Third-party inventory and external gates

Status: engineering inventory; license and legal conclusions require independent review (`EXTERNAL_GATE`).

Python packages are pinned/resolved by `uv.lock`; names and versions are represented by SBOM evidence. `sgp4` 2.27 is the current propagation library and remains subject to scientific qualification boundaries. NASA and CelesTrak are future data-source candidates: no live NASA data is activated or represented as qualified. Oya is a future provider-neutral voice candidate and remains disabled, non-billable and network-free. Provider SDKs, models, data licenses, subprocessors, residency, training use, pricing and exit terms must be assessed before adoption.
